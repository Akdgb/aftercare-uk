# AfterCare UK: pre-launch compliance and content audit

Date: 19 November 2026. Target launch: 20 November 2026. Scope: codebase on branch `claude/modest-lamport-pof0tq` (based on `main` at merge of PR #11), local build and browser tests, database schema, and the owner's dashboard screenshots shared in session (Vercel, Resend, GoDaddy).

**Limits of this audit.** The deployed site could not be reached from the audit environment, so live checks are marked NOT VERIFIED unless the owner supplied a screenshot. Automated tests and code review are not legal certification. A short review by a UK data protection solicitor is recommended before partnering with councils.

## 5. DPIA screening and draft DPIA
Screening (ICO criteria): special category data (faith, ethnic origin, possibly health in notes): yes. Vulnerable people (bereaved, possibly young people aged 16 to 17): yes. Data shared with other people (family plans): yes. Large scale: no. Innovative technology or AI: no. Automated decisions: no. Outcome: **a DPIA should be completed** because special category data and vulnerable people are combined.

## 7. Data subject requests
Channel: privacy@aftercareuk.co.uk (forwarded by Resend to the owner). Steps: log the request the same day; confirm identity proportionately (reply from the account email); respond within one calendar month (extend by up to two months for complex requests, telling the person within the first month); export by querying the user's rows and sending a JSON or PDF copy; never disclose other members' details without a lawful reason; record the outcome. Keep the log privately (not in this repository) with: date received, requester, type, identity check, deadline, outcome, date closed.

## 8. Incident response and safeguarding
Detect: Vercel and Resend alerts, user reports, unusual sign-in volumes. Contain: rotate `SESSION_SECRET` (signs everyone out), rotate the Resend key and database password, disable affected features. Assess: what data, how many people, likely harm. Record every personal data breach in a private log. Report to the ICO within 72 hours of becoming aware if it is likely to result in a risk to people's rights; tell affected people without undue delay if the risk is high. Concerning messages: if an email suggests someone is at immediate risk, reply with 999 and Samaritans (116 123) details; AfterCare does not monitor notes or provide counselling. Keep the ICO breach line, provider support links and an adviser's contact in a private document.

## 9. Children and online safety (draft assessments)
Children's Code: public guidance pages can be read by under-18s. No profiling, no tracking, no advertising, high privacy by default, accounts 16+ with a neutral age question. Likely low risk; keep this record. Online Safety Act: shared plans allow invited members to post notes visible to each other, so AfterCare is likely a user-to-user service. Illegal content risk: low (private, invite-only groups of family members, no public posting, no search, no images or files). Children's access: accounts are 16+, but 16 to 17 year olds can join a family plan, so the service is likely to be accessed by children; risk of harmful content is low because content is limited to family members chosen by the owner. Controls: owner can remove members; members can leave; report concerns to hello@. Owner to review these assessments against Ofcom's current guidance, keep them on file, and revisit yearly or when features change. Alternative if not comfortable: turn off task notes until reviewed.
